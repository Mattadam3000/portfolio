MATT ADAM / WORK

I make paintings, objects and work in public about control: what controls us, what we try to control, and who gets to decide.

WORK ↓
01SKINNYOZEMPIC / BODY CONTROL

OZEMPIC / BODY CONTROL

SKINNY

Warhol used the soup can because it said something about his time. I use the Ozempic needle because I think it says something about ours.

We try to control our bodies, our appetite, how we look and how people see us. At the same time, drugs, algorithms and the world around us are constantly shaping what we want.

02PAINTINGSPAINTINGS / SCREENPRINT / PHOTOGRAPH

PAINTINGS / SCREENPRINT / PHOTOGRAPH

PAINTINGS

The public work gets the first reaction. The paintings are where the pressure stays.

Screenprinting and painting over my own photographs, then pushing the same images back into public space. Different forms, same obsession: control.

03OBJECTSAPPETITE / STATUS / SELF-MANAGEMENT

APPETITE / STATUS / SELF-MANAGEMENT

OBJECTS

Objects that make hidden systems physical.

An empty bag of cocaine. A piece of raw meat. An Ozempic needle. Zines, bandanas and sculptural publishing made to look like the things they are talking about.

04UNWRAP & STEALPUBLIC WORK / ACQUISITION SYSTEM

PUBLIC WORK / ACQUISITION SYSTEM

UNWRAP & STEAL

I wrap original artworks like gifts, put them up in cities around the world, release clues, and tell people they can steal them.

People hunt, race, trade information and take the work. The finding becomes part of the object's history.

05PUBLIC WORKINTERVENTIONS / ACCESS / SPECTACLE

INTERVENTIONS / ACCESS / SPECTACLE

PUBLIC WORK

The conditions around the work are part of the work.

For my first warehouse exhibition, a 2,000-pound concrete monolith blocked the gate with a painting buried inside it. The audience had to break through the block to enter the show.
