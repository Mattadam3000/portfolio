Unwrap
& Steal

REAL LIFE ART HEISTS

Over 100 million views. Three documentaries, one directed by Sam Lipman-Stern of the Emmy-nominated Telemarketers. Matt Adam wraps original artworks like gifts, hides them in public, and releases clues. People race to find and steal them. Ownership moves through attention, speed, and the will to go get it.

作品 — THE PRACTICE

Objects

APPETITE / STATUS / SELF-MANAGEMENT

Photographs of objects, made into zines and bandanas, built to look real. An empty bag of cocaine. A piece of raw meat. An Ozempic needle. Each one makes a hidden system visible: appetite, status and body control.

01

An oversized dollar bill and cocaine staged on the street. Money as appetite, status and the performance of excess.

03

SKINNY

On
The News

Andy Warhol had soup cans. Matt Adam has the Ozempic needle. Warhol was interested in what America consumed. Matt Adam is interested in what people use to control themselves. The Ozempic intervention broke past the art world and onto NBC News.

NBC NEWS — TAP TO PLAY

Nobody Came
To My Art Show

ART SHOW + PERFORMANCE ART

Weeks before his first warehouse exhibition, Matt Adam ran through Hollywood in a clown costume spray-painting "nobody came to my art show" on a pink-wrapped canvas. Invitations arrived as pieces of concrete marked "break." On opening night, a 2,000-pound concrete monolith blocked the gate with the canvas buried inside. The crowd had to break it open to enter.

Paintings

Matt Adam takes familiar images, objects and symbols from popular culture and changes their context. Different mediums, same questions: what controls us, what we try to control, and who gets to decide.

—ABOUT概要

Matt Adam is a visual architect based in Los Angeles, from Toronto, working across photography, art, creative direction, objects, and public interventions. His work is built around control: appetite, status, self-optimization, and the systems shaping what people want. He built visual worlds for Future and Metro Boomin, Juice WRLD, The Weeknd, and Benny Blanco; created Unwrap & Steal; and has work featured by NBC News, Taschen, and Beyond the Streets.

Who shot the Juice WRLD Legends Never Die cover?

Matt Adam shot the Juice WRLD Legends Never Die cover. He photographed Juice WRLD in 2019; the family selected the image for the posthumous album, which debuted at #1 with 497,000 first-week units.

Who built the visual identity for We Don't Trust You?

Matt Adam built the visual identity for Future and Metro Boomin's We Don't Trust You and We Still Don't Trust You, covering art, vinyl, and the full rollout. Both debuted at #1 in 2024.

What is Unwrap & Steal?

Unwrap & Steal is a public art project by Matt Adam. He wraps original artworks like gifts, hides them in public, and releases clues so people race to find and keep them.
