MATT ADAM / WORK

I make paintings, objects, performance art and work in public about control: what controls us, what we try to control, and who gets to decide.

01SKINNY CHRISTOZEMPIC / BODY CONTROL

OZEMPIC / BODY CONTROL

SKINNY CHRIST

Warhol used the soup can because it said something about his time. I use the Ozempic needle because I think it says something about ours.

We try to control our bodies, our appetite, how we look and how people see us. At the same time, drugs, algorithms and the world around us are constantly shaping what we want.

02PAINTINGSPAINTINGS / SCREENPRINT / PHOTOGRAPH

PAINTINGS / SCREENPRINT / PHOTOGRAPH

PAINTINGS

My painting practice moves across subjects, but the questions stay the same: control, belief, appetite, and what we choose to worship.

The works below are from Skinny Christ—Ozempic needles arranged as crosses, turning a tool of body control into an image of faith, discipline and self-transformation.

03OBJECTSAPPETITE / STATUS / SELF-MANAGEMENT

APPETITE / STATUS / SELF-MANAGEMENT

OBJECTS

Objects that make hidden systems physical.

An empty bag of cocaine. Raw meat. An Ozempic needle. Zines, bandanas and sculptural publishing that turn appetite, status, consumption and body control into physical things.

01

PARTY

An oversized dollar bill and cocaine staged on the street. Money as appetite, status and the performance of excess.

02

ROTTEN

A deer head and trail of blood staged in the street. The Rotten zine and bandana carry the image into objects, with the zine wrapped and displayed like a cut of raw meat in a butcher’s case.

03

SKINNY

The Ozempic image moved from public intervention into printed objects, zines and bandanas.

04UNWRAP & STEALPUBLIC WORK / ACQUISITION SYSTEM

PUBLIC WORK / ACQUISITION SYSTEM

UNWRAP & STEAL

I wrap original artworks like gifts, put them up in cities around the world, release clues, and people steal them.

People hunt for the clue and race to be the first to steal the work. The chase, the theft and who ends up with it become part of the piece.

05PUBLIC WORKINTERVENTIONS / PERFORMANCE / PUBLIC SPACE

INTERVENTIONS / PERFORMANCE / PUBLIC SPACE

PUBLIC WORK

The conditions around the work are part of the work.

I use public space as a material: street installations, performance art, exhibitions, objects and Unwrap & Steal. Location, access, risk, spectacle and what people do in response are part of the work—not just the object at the center.
